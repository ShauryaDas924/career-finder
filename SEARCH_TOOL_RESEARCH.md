# Search Tool Research

Research verified on **2026-09-29** for the compact “Search your way” guidance section. This is a dated product-research record, not a live ranking or a claim that every external listing is complete.

## Scope and decision rule

The section needed to answer two practical student intents without turning Whoopberry into a job board:

1. find opportunities by work setup, location, and job type; and
2. narrow opportunities by pay or compensation.

The final section links to **four canonical catalog records**: Handshake and Himalayas for work setup/location, then HiringCafe and USAJOBS Early Careers for compensation. HiringCafe and Himalayas were added to the full catalog after the deeper review. Simplify remains a featured catalog resource with verified salary metadata and aliases, but HiringCafe subsumes its compensation-filter role in this compact section while USAJOBS adds a distinct federal specialist. The catalog now contains **64 resources**, **13 categories**, and **5 featured resources**. The `Resource` schema is unchanged.

The feature still adds no hosted listings, scraping, ranking, application flow, persistence, account system, or backend. External destinations own their filters, accounts, prices, listings, and application flows.

## Salary and location distinctions

“Shows salary,” “filters by salary,” and “sorts by salary” are different capabilities:

| Destination | Salary display/filter behavior | Genuine salary ordering | Location + salary in one search? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Handshake | May display estimated or employer-provided compensation; selected primarily for work setup/location. | No verified salary sort. | Location is strong; salary is not the reason for selection. |
| Himalayas | Minimum/maximum multi-currency range and salary-required toggle. | **Salary (high - low)** and **Salary (low - high)**. | **Yes, for remote work.** Country/time-zone eligibility can be combined with salary controls. |
| HiringCafe | Numeric minimum salary and transparent-pay-only filter. | **Highest salary** and **Lowest salary**. | **Yes.** It has the strongest broad combination of precise geography, work setting, job type/level, and pay controls reviewed. |
| USAJOBS | Minimum pay filter. | **Highest salary** for federal results. | **Yes, for federal work.** Location/distance and work-schedule controls can be combined with minimum pay. |
| Simplify | Salary filter and saved searches above a target range. | No verified high-to-low sort. | Yes, through location plus salary filters, but HiringCafe covers this role with stronger transparency and ordering controls. |

The deeper pass therefore found genuine salary ordering in **HiringCafe, Himalayas, and USAJOBS**. HiringCafe and USAJOBS are the focused compensation pair because one is broad and one is an authoritative federal specialist. Himalayas remains selected under work setup because its remote country/time-zone controls are the distinct reason to open it.

## Deep research check

1. **What hidden or niche resources were found?** HiringCafe, Himalayas, YouSourced, SpeedyApply's and Dreamwork's maintained student trackers, InternAtlas, and Neuronify's NYC AI board were the strongest lesser-known findings.
2. **Did anything beat Handshake for work-location filtering?** Not as an overall student-first destination: Handshake retains the best school- and student-oriented role. HiringCafe has stronger broad compound filters, and Himalayas is stronger for remote country/time-zone eligibility, so both add distinct capability rather than fully replacing it.
3. **Did anything beat Simplify for compensation filtering?** Yes. HiringCafe adds broad minimum-pay and disclosed-pay filtering plus explicit highest/lowest salary ordering; Himalayas is stronger for the same need within remote-only work.
4. **Did anything beat USAJOBS for genuine salary-high-to-low sorting?** Yes for broader coverage: HiringCafe exposes **Highest salary**, and Himalayas exposes **Salary (high - low)** for remote roles. USAJOBS remains selected because it is the authoritative federal specialist with its own **Highest salary** sort.
5. **Was a strong resource combining both location and salary found?** Yes. HiringCafe does so broadly, Himalayas does so for remote country/time-zone searches, and USAJOBS does so for federal roles.
6. **Did any GitHub repository or small tracker deserve inclusion?** No. The best were maintained but table-based, technology-heavy, and lacked pay filtering/sorting; existing catalog tracker coverage is already strong.
7. **What is the final selected set, and why?** Handshake and Himalayas cover student-specific and remote-specialist work-location discovery; HiringCafe and USAJOBS cover broad and federal compensation discovery. Together they stay small, add distinct capabilities, and preserve Whoopberry as a resource organizer rather than a job board.
